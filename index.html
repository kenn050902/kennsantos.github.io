Working approach

From raw data to useful insight.

I focus on understanding the business question first, then building reporting that is reliable, easy to use, and practical to maintain.

01

Discover & define

Understand the reporting goals, users, KPIs, data sources, and current pain points.

02

Review & prepare data

Assess source quality, transform data, validate business rules, and shape the reporting model.

03

Build & validate

Develop dashboards or workflows, test calculations, and review outputs with stakeholders.

04

Handoff & improve

Document key logic, support adoption, and identify opportunities for ongoing improvement.
